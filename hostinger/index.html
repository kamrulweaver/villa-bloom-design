The property

The newest villa in the Jakes collection, on Jamaica's quiet south coast

Seaweed sits on an acre and a half above the water in Calabash Bay, ten minutes from Jakes proper. Two buildings — a four-bedroom main villa and a two-bedroom sister villa — share the gardens, the pool and the staff. Hire the whole property, or just one house.

Inside, dark woods and organic furnishings made locally by Island Art Jamaica meet Jakes' signature mosaic tiles, shell adornments and coloured bottles set into the walls.

Eco-conscience

Designed to sit lightly on this coastline

Seaweed was architecturally designed to be sympathetic to its natural environment — pioneering a standard of luxury living with an eco-conscience on Jamaica's south coast.

  • Solar hot water
  • Wind-generated power
  • LED and day lighting
  • Reclaimed lumber
  • Natural river stone
  • Rainwater harvesting
  • Grey water to irrigation
  • Local adaptive planting
